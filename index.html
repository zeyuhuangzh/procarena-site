ProcArena

A Multi-Scenario Benchmark for LLMs on Direct and Interactive PL/SQL Development from Natural Language

Real database development is not one prompt and one answer. ProcArena spans synthesis (A1–A5) and evolution (B1–B4) of stored procedures, and plays every task in two modes: Direct, with the full requirement up front, and Interactive, where the model must interrogate a simulated user and the live database to earn the requirement back — under a question budget, with a library-of-context fallback. One criterion throughout: execution-state equivalence (EX). Every cell below opens the full trace of a real episode. Accepted at VLDB 2027.
